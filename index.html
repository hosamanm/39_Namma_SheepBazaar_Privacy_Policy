Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Namma SheepBazaar ("Namma SheepBazaar", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Namma SheepBazaar mobile application (the "App").

Namma SheepBazaar is a livestock marketplace application that allows customers to browse sheep listings, save favourite sheep, submit sheep for sale, manage sell requests, explore exchange opportunities, and manage related customer activities.

Important: This policy must match the data practices and permissions enabled in the released version of the App. Remove any section that does not apply to the production version.

1. Information We May Collect

1.1 Account and Profile Information

When you create or use an account, we may collect information needed to create and manage your profile, such as:

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number
  • City, district, state, or other location information that you provide
  • Profile photograph, if you choose to upload one
  • Authentication-related account information

1.2 Sheep and Livestock Information

If you browse, submit, sell, or exchange livestock through the App, we may process information you provide about sheep, including:

  • Breed
  • Gender
  • Age
  • Weight
  • Colour
  • Health or vaccination information that you provide
  • Location or livestock-related location details that you provide
  • Price or expected price
  • Photographs or other livestock media you upload
  • Description and other details you enter

1.3 Enquiries, Sell Requests and Exchange Information

When you submit an enquiry, sell request, exchange request, or related activity, we may collect the information required to process and manage that request, including the details you submit and its status and history.

1.4 Documents

If the App provides a document-upload or “My Documents” feature, you may choose to upload documents required for the relevant livestock or account process. Such documents may contain personal information. Only upload documents necessary for the intended purpose.

1.5 Device and Technical Information

Depending on the features and services enabled in the released App, we may receive limited technical information such as device type, operating-system information, App version, diagnostics, crash information, and identifiers used to maintain security and service functionality.

1.6 Notifications

If push notifications are enabled, the App may process a device or notification token so that relevant account, request, or service notifications can be delivered to your device.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts

  • Displaying available sheep and livestock listings
  • Processing sell requests and exchange requests
  • Managing enquiries, visits, appointments, and customer activities
  • Saving and displaying wishlist items
  • Managing uploaded profile, livestock, and supporting information
  • Sending relevant service and account notifications

  • Complying with applicable legal obligations
